      This tool estimates ATS compatibility and hiring readiness for guidance only. It never invents facts about you, and it can't guarantee that any specific ATS will parse your résumé a certain way, or that you'll get an interview or offer.

      How it works

      Your résumé is parsed and scored entirely in your browser using a fixed 100-point rubric: parsability, contact info, summary, experience quality, skills/keywords, education/projects/certifications, writing quality, and hiring readiness. If you add a job description or pick a role, keyword matching and project suggestions become role- or job-specific.

      Privacy

      Nothing you paste or upload leaves your browser — there is no server component. Refreshing the page clears everything.
